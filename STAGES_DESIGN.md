# Escape Mr. Taqueria - Stage Design Guide (ACTUAL)

## Overview
25 progressively harder stages with custom mechanics, creatures, puzzles, and combat. Checkpoints after each stage.

---

## 🍳 KITCHEN STAGES (1-5)

### Stage 1: Simple Blocks Jump
**Difficulty:** ⭐ (Easiest)
- **Layout:** 4 blocks in a line over lava
- **Mechanics:** Basic jumping across platforms
- **Obstacles:** Lava below (instant death if you fall)
- **Goal:** Jump across all 4 blocks to reach the exit
- **Checkpoint:** After stage 1
- **Notes:** Tutorial-like introduction

### Stage 2: Swinging Axe Platform Walk
**Difficulty:** ⭐⭐
- **Layout:** Platform with 3 swinging axes coming down
- **Mechanics:** Timing - walk under swinging axes without getting hit
- **Obstacles:** 3 single swinging axes on timer
- **Hazard:** Lava below the platform (visible after axes)
- **Goal:** Walk across platform, dodge 3 axes, reach the exit
- **Checkpoint:** After stage 2
- **Notes:** Introduction to timing mechanics

### Stage 3: Giant Parkour Room Climb
**Difficulty:** ⭐⭐⭐
- **Layout:** Massive vertical room filled with parkour platforms
- **Mechanics:** Jumping, climbing, wall-jumping
- **Obstacles:** Multiple levels of platforms to navigate upward
- **Goal:** Climb from bottom to top of the giant room
- **Checkpoint:** After stage 3
- **Notes:** Major vertical challenge - very huge room

### Stage 4: Moving Platform with Statue
**Difficulty:** ⭐⭐⭐
- **Layout:** Moving platforms that go back and forth
- **Mechanics:** Jumping on moving platforms
- **Obstacles:** Platforms move back-and-forth (statue moves them or they're mechanized)
- **Goal:** Ride/jump on moving platforms to reach exit
- **Checkpoint:** After stage 4
- **Notes:** Introduction to moving platform mechanics

### Stage 5: Dark Room with Taco Creatures
**Difficulty:** ⭐⭐⭐⭐
- **Layout:** Dark room with many taco mini creatures
- **Mechanics:** Combat/dodging + navigation
- **Obstacles:** Bunch of taco minis trying to attack you
- **Goal:** Find the exit door in the dark while avoiding creatures
- **Checkpoint:** After stage 5
- **Notes:** First creature encounter - dark atmosphere

---

## 📦 STORAGE/PUZZLE STAGES (6-10)

### Stage 6: Key & Door Puzzle
**Difficulty:** ⭐⭐⭐⭐
- **Layout:** Room with locked door and hidden key
- **Mechanics:** Platforming + puzzle solving
- **Obstacles:** Need to find key first, then unlock door
- **Puzzle:** Color sequence - door tells you colors in order, you must do them correctly
- **Goal:** Find key → unlock door → solve color puzzle → exit
- **Checkpoint:** After stage 6
- **Notes:** Introduces puzzle elements

### Stage 7: Diagonal Moving Blocks
**Difficulty:** ⭐⭐⭐⭐
- **Layout:** Blocks arranged diagonally across the room
- **Mechanics:** Precise jumping
- **Obstacles:** Blocks move back-and-forth diagonally
- **Goal:** Jump on moving diagonal blocks to reach exit
- **Checkpoint:** After stage 7
- **Notes:** Challenging diagonal precision jumping

### Stage 8: Disappearing Platform Stairs
**Difficulty:** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
- **Layout:** Platforms stacked like stairs going up
- **Mechanics:** Speed + jumping
- **Obstacles:** Blocks disappear after you jump on them
- **Goal:** Jump up the disappearing stairs quickly before they're all gone
- **Checkpoint:** After stage 8
- **Notes:** Requires fast reflexes

### Stage 9: Disappearing Platforms
**Difficulty:** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
- **Layout:** Series of platforms that disappear
- **Mechanics:** Quick platforming
- **Obstacles:** Platforms vanish - must keep moving
- **Goal:** Cross disappearing platforms quickly
- **Checkpoint:** After stage 9
- **Notes:** Similar to Stage 8 but variation

### Stage 10: Bouncy Trampoline Jump
**Difficulty:** ⭐⭐⭐⭐
- **Layout:** Giant open room with bouncy trampoline at bottom
- **Mechanics:** Bounce mechanics + platforming
- **Obstacles:** Jump to bouncy thing, bounce off to platforms
- **Goal:** Use bouncy thing to reach the exit platform
- **Checkpoint:** After stage 10
- **Notes:** Introduction to bouncy/spring mechanics

---

## 🎯 COMBAT & CHALLENGE STAGES (11-15)

### Stage 11: Random Disappearing Rows (Lava Roulette)
**Difficulty:** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
- **Layout:** 5 different rows of platforms with lava below
- **Mechanics:** Walking across rows - some disappear if you step on them
- **Obstacles:** Randomized each time - ghostly platforms that make you fall to lava
  - 4 rows: unsafe (disappear when stepped on)
  - 1 row: safe (hard platform you can walk on)
- **Goal:** Find the safe row and cross without falling to lava
- **Hazard:** Lava below kills you
- **Checkpoint:** After stage 11
- **Notes:** Luck/memory element - randomized each attempt

### Stage 12: Taco Creature Combat
**Difficulty:** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
- **Layout:** Battle arena room
- **Mechanics:** Combat with taco machine gun
- **Obstacles:** 3 giant/medium taco creatures attacking
- **Weapons:** Taco machine gun (given to you)
- **Goal:** Kill all 3 creatures using the taco machine gun
- **Checkpoint:** After stage 12
- **Notes:** First real combat stage

### Stage 13: Taco Assembly Puzzle
**Difficulty:** ⭐⭐⭐⭐
- **Layout:** 3 different sections with taco ingredients
- **Mechanics:** Platforming to each section + puzzle
- **Obstacles:** 3 taco ingredients to collect/interact with
- **Puzzle:** Each section tells you how it wants the taco made
  - Go to each section
  - It tells you the ingredient order
  - Assemble taco correctly with given ingredients
- **Goal:** Make the taco the right way, deliver it to exit
- **Checkpoint:** After stage 13
- **Notes:** Creative puzzle stage

### Stage 14: Stages 1+2+3 Combined
**Difficulty:** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
- **Layout:** Combination room with all elements from stages 1-3
- **Mechanics:** All previous mechanics combined
- **Obstacles:** 4 blocks to jump + swinging axes + climbing
- **Goal:** Navigate all challenges in sequence
- **Checkpoint:** After stage 14
- **Notes:** Major difficulty spike - boss-like stage

### Stage 15: Stages 4+5+6 Combined
**Difficulty:** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
- **Layout:** Combination room with all elements from stages 4-6
- **Mechanics:** Moving platforms + creatures + puzzles
- **Obstacles:** Moving platforms + dark creatures + door puzzle
- **Goal:** Complete all three challenges in one stage
- **Checkpoint:** After stage 15
- **Notes:** Complex multi-challenge stage

---

## 🏢 ADVANCED STAGES (16-20)

### Stage 16: Stages 7+8+9 Combined
**Difficulty:** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
- **Layout:** Combination of diagonal blocks, disappearing stairs, and disappearing platforms
- **Mechanics:** Jumping + speed + precision
- **Obstacles:** Moving diagonal blocks + disappearing stairs + platform phases
- **Goal:** Navigate all three challenges quickly
- **Checkpoint:** After stage 16
- **Notes:** Speed and precision test

### Stage 17: Platform-Bouncy-Code Room
**Difficulty:** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
- **Layout:** Multi-section room with platforms, bouncy, and code lock door
- **Mechanics:** Platforming + bouncing + puzzle solving
- **Process:**
  1. Jump on platform to platform to platform
  2. Jump to bouncy thing and bounce back up to second platform
  3. Find a code hidden in the room
  4. Reach the door platform
  5. Enter code into door lock to open it
- **Goal:** Solve and progress through all sections
- **Checkpoint:** After stage 17
- **Notes:** Multi-part stage with code puzzle

### Stage 18: Spinning Circle Platform Ride
**Difficulty:** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
- **Layout:** Circle with rectangle pieces rotating around it
- **Mechanics:** Jumping on moving platforms in circular pattern
- **Obstacles:** Platforms rotate in circles - must jump on and ride them
  - Jump on one rectangle piece
  - It spins you around to the next piece
  - Jump to the next spinning piece
  - Continue the pattern to reach exit
- **Goal:** Ride the spinning circle platforms to the exit
- **Checkpoint:** After stage 18
- **Notes:** Unique rotating mechanic

### Stage 19: Stages 8+9+10 Combined
**Difficulty:** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
- **Layout:** Combination of disappearing stairs, disappearing platforms, and bouncy mechanics
- **Mechanics:** Speed + timing + bouncing
- **Obstacles:** All three stage types combined
- **Goal:** Navigate the combined challenges
- **Checkpoint:** After stage 19
- **Notes:** Fast-paced combined stage

### Stage 20: Stages 11+12+13 Combined
**Difficulty:** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
- **Layout:** Large room combining lava rows, combat, and taco assembly
- **Mechanics:** Navigation + combat + puzzle
- **Process:**
  1. Cross the dangerous disappearing rows
  2. Fight taco creatures with machine gun
  3. Collect taco ingredients and assemble correctly
  4. Escape through exit
- **Goal:** Complete all three challenges
- **Checkpoint:** After stage 20
- **Notes:** Major challenge combining multiple mechanics

## 🎮 EXTREME STAGES (21-24)

### Stage 21: Platforms with Moving Souls/Obstacles
**Difficulty:** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
- **Layout:** Platforms that move back and forth with ghostly obstacles
- **Mechanics:** Jumping on moving platforms while dodging souls
- **Obstacles:** Souls/obstacles moving back-and-forth between platforms
- **Goal:** Jump from platform to platform avoiding the moving souls
- **Checkpoint:** After stage 21
- **Notes:** Advanced timing challenge

### Stage 22: Stages 14+15+16 Combined
**Difficulty:** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
- **Layout:** Massive combination of all early combined stages
- **Mechanics:** All mechanics from stages 1-9 combined
- **Process:** Navigate through blocks, axes, climbing, moving platforms, creatures, puzzles, diagonal blocks, disappearing blocks
- **Goal:** Complete the ultimate early-stage challenge
- **Checkpoint:** After stage 22
- **Notes:** Extreme difficulty - tests all basic mechanics

### Stage 23: Taco Assembly Master (Make it Right or Die)
**Difficulty:** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
- **Layout:** Special taco assembly room with high stakes
- **Mechanics:** Puzzle + platforming + consequence
- **Process:**
  1. Get shown how customers want their tacos made
  2. Make the taco EXACTLY the way they like it
  3. If you mess up even slightly = INSTANT DEATH
  4. Respawn at checkpoint and retry
- **Goal:** Make perfect tacos for different customers
- **Checkpoint:** Multiple checkpoints within this stage
- **Notes:** High-pressure stage - one wrong move and you die

### Stage 24: Stages 17+18+19 Combined
**Difficulty:** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
- **Layout:** Complex combination of code rooms, spinning circles, and platform challenges
- **Mechanics:** All advanced mechanics combined
- **Process:** Navigate platforms → bounce mechanics → code puzzle → spinning circles → escape
- **Goal:** Complete the ultimate advanced challenge
- **Checkpoint:** After stage 24
- **Notes:** Second-to-last stage - extremely difficult

## 🚪 FINAL BOSS STAGE (25)

### Stage 25: MR. TAQUERIA FINAL BOSS FIGHT
**Difficulty:** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ (EXTREME)

**Boss Description:**
- **Name:** Mr. Taqueria
- **Appearance:** Giant taco dude with apron on, taco face
- **Arena:** Final battle room

**Battle Mechanics:**
1. **Phase 1 - Legs & Arms:**
   - Mr. Taqueria stands in arena with visible legs and arms
   - You're given a TACO MACHINE GUN
   - Shoot the legs and arms off one by one
   - Each limb has its own health bar
   - Dodge Mr. Taqueria's attacks while shooting

2. **Boss Attacks:**
   - Uses admin powers on you:
     - 🥶 FREEZE you in place
     - ✈️ MAKE YOU FLY uncontrollably
     - 💥 FLING you across the arena
     - 🎯 Other special attacks

3. **Victory Condition:**
   - Shoot off all 4 limbs (2 legs + 2 arms)
   - Defeat Mr. Taqueria completely

4. **After Victory:**
   - **ESCAPE!** You run to a car
   - Drive to the finish line
   - Get a bunch of cool items as rewards
   - **GAME WON!** 🎉

**Checkpoint:** Checkpoint at start of boss fight

**Notes:** 
- Ultimate challenge combining combat and dodging
- Uses all admin powers as attacks
- Dramatic escape sequence after boss dies
- Epic finale with car chase/escape
- Victory rewards players with cool items

## 🎯 Checkpoint Strategy

**Checkpoint after EVERY stage** so players don't lose too much progress.

**Extra checkpoints within:**
- Stage 23 (taco assembly - high pressure)
- Stage 25 (boss fight - complex battle)

---

## 💡 Implementation Tips

1. **Test difficulty curve** - Make sure each combined stage feels like a natural progression
2. **Visual clarity** - Show stage numbers, objectives clearly
3. **Audio cues** - Different sounds for different mechanics
4. **Mr. Taqueria design** - Make the final boss visually impressive with clear weak points (legs/arms)
5. **Victory celebration** - Make the car escape scene epic and rewarding
6. **Machine gun mechanics** - Easy to aim, satisfying to use
7. **Checkpoint system** - Quick respawn after death
